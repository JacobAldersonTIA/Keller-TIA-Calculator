Calculation method: 10% of the original allotment is retained by the district. The remaining 90% covers employee gross compensation plus the employer costs listed above. Each employer cost is estimated as a percentage of employee gross compensation; employee deductions are then estimated as percentages of employee gross compensation. Federal withholding is approximated as 22% of gross compensation, without adjusting the taxable wage base for pre-tax deductions. The deduction percentages are based on estimates confirmed by Keller ISD Payroll; actual withholding and payment amounts may vary.

All calculations occur in your browser. This calculator does not save or submit the award amount, and it uses no cookies, analytics, database, browser storage, or external scripts. Standard website-hosting requests and logs may still occur.
